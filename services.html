Category 06 • Specialized Practice

Negotiable Instruments (NI) Act Matters

Dedicated Section 138 cheque dishonour, recovery, and defence documentation.

06

Cheque dishonour under Section 138 of the Negotiable Instruments Act is subject to strict statutory limitation windows. A delay of even a single day in issuing a demand notice can extinguish your criminal remedy. We treat NI Act matters as a dedicated, standalone practice — providing rapid notice drafting, strategic defence preparation, and litigation management.

Section 138 Statutory Demand Notices: Precise legal notice drafting within the mandatory 30-day window following cheque return memo receipt.
Strategic Reply Drafting: Drafting compelling replies establishing defence against misuse of security cheques, discharge of liability, or fraud.
Criminal Complaint Documentation: Drafting formal complaints under Section 138 / 142 NI Act for filing before competent Judicial Magistrates.
Evidence & Affidavits: Drafting complainant evidence affidavits under Section 145 NI Act and applications for 20% interim compensation under Section 143A.
Defence & Cross-Examination Strategy: Comprehensive defence dossiers rebutting statutory presumptions under Section 118 and 139 of the Act.
Compounding & Settlement Agreements: Drafting consent terms, compromise petitions, and Lok Adalat settlement documentation.
Counsel Coordination: Filing and in-person court representation are conducted through qualified advocates enrolled with the relevant State Bar Councils.
